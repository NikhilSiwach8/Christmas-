0. WHAT WE LEARNED FROM THE REFERENCE VIDEO (structure to mirror)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Scene composition (1280x720 frame, in order of importance):
  1. Pale, bone-coloured background with soft scalloped hills; small props
     scattered (crosses, skulls) and heavy chains hanging from the top corners.
  2. Hand-drawn 5x4 grid scratched on the ground in red ink, drawn in slight
     perspective (wider at the bottom), with ink splatter dots. NOT a framed box.
  3. Wheel on a pole ABOVE the board, centre-top, with an eye in its hub, spiked
     rim, a creature limb curling around it; a stone basin on each side of it.
  4. Groups of small hooded followers (black cloaks, glowing eyes) flank the
     board left and right; a torn banner with an eye emblem behind each group.
  5. Candles on the four corners of the board.
  6. Logo top-left (small, outlined lettering with an eye replacing a letter).
  7. Bottom bar: compact, dark, rounded; left = menu avatar button + hamburger;
     centre = BALANCE | WIN | BET (small coloured label over white value);
     right = + / - stacked, a big round spin button with circular arrow, and
     two small round buttons (Auto "A", Turbo lightning).
  8. Symbols: 5 cartoon characters + card ranks A K Q J 10 hand-lettered in
     the same ink style; ranks use an eye as decoration; characters have a
     tiny shadow ellipse and a thick black outline.
  9. Intro screen: logo + three dark torn-edge cards with a title and 3-4 lines
     of text each, "PRESS TO CONTINUE" below.
 10. Transition: black ink blobs with big glowing eyes swallow the screen.
 11. Menu / buy screen: paper cards (title, small illustration, candle-style
     slider, price, red button) for: Ante Spins, The Offering, and three Scatter
     buys; a small paper "BET - / + / BALANCE" plaque with leaf corners.
 12. Typography: hand-drawn brush-gothic logo; card titles in an uncial/
     blackletter-flavoured caps; body text in heavy condensed caps; bar values
     in a bold rounded sans.

What we MIRROR: layout, proportions, hierarchy, UI conventions, ink-cartoon look,
cute-but-eerie tone.
What we DO NOT COPY (must be original): the creatures, the cult/occult motifs
(skulls, crosses, tentacle, pentagrams, candles, blood), the eye-in-the-O logo
lettering, the exact poses, the colours (red/black), the avatar icon. Every
prompt below uses ICE equivalents. Never upload frames of the reference into an
image generator and never name the reference game/studio in a prompt.

1. LOCKED STYLE BIBLE (never change between assets)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Mood: cute-but-eerie winter ritual. A snowbound shrine where little frost spirits
perform a ceremony. Quirky, readable, slightly mischievous. NOT scary, NOT gory.

PALETTE (only these, plus tints/shades of them):
  Snow-bone ground ........ #F3EFE6   (main background)
  Paper ................... #E9E2D0   (cards, plaques)
  Ink black ............... #14141C   (ALL outlines)
  Frost cyan (main accent)  #3FC8F4   (replaces the reference's red)
  Deep ice blue ........... #1F5FA8
  Pale frost .............. #DFF6FF
  Aurora violet ........... #8F6BFF   (specials: wild, wheel, multipliers)
  Warm gold (rare) ........ #F2C14E   (premium trim, spin-ring, coin only)
  Night navy (UI bar) ..... #0E1B2E
  Pointer coral ........... #FF5A6E   (wheel pointer ONLY)
  Forbidden: crimson red, orange, brown-black "horror" palette, neon green.

LINE: thick, slightly wobbly black ink outline (about 6 px on a 1024 px asset),
  rounded joins, outline thicker on the outside silhouette than inside details.
SHADING: flat cel colour + ONE soft darker tone + ONE small white highlight
  blob/stripe. No gradients, no glow blur (glow = flat translucent cyan shape).
LIGHT: top-left. Every standing character has a small dark flat ellipse shadow
  beneath it (#14141C at 25%).
TEXTURE: faint paper grain over everything (very subtle).
PROPORTIONS: characters = big round head, tiny body, stubby limbs, glowing cyan
  eyes (flat cyan oval with a white highlight dot). Slightly asymmetrical.
RECURRING EMBLEM ("Frost Eye Sigil"): a six-point snowflake whose centre is a
  vertical almond-shaped eye with a cyan iris. It appears on: logo, banners,
  wheel hub, scatter, buttons, cards. Always drawn in ink with a cyan iris.
RUNES: simple angular straight-stroke marks, decorative only, never words.
